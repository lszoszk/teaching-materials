2 · UN Travaux Préparatoires · Origin

6 June 1952: nine votes to eight

The French amendment (E/CN.4/L.201) to insert the words “in a democratic society” after “necessary” was adopted by 9 votes to 8, with 1 abstention.

E/CN.4/SR.325, p. 20

Roosevelt (United States) opposed the words because of their ambiguity (p. 15)

Cassin (France): a reference to public order and to democratic society was essential to safeguard the freedoms (pp. 11–12)

Summary records paraphrase in the third person: check the scan before you quote.

How we work today

Predict, build, check

1

Work in pairs: one writes the request, the other checks. Swap for the next app.

2

While the model writes (1 to 4 minutes), write down what a correct result should show.

3

Check one number by hand against the source: the app may run and still be wrong.

4

Look inside: Run it three times (the same numbers every time?), What left this laptop.

5

An error in red: send it back to the model in the same conversation. Three tries without progress: call me.

Session 4 · your own idea

A good request says what goes in, what comes out and how to check it

The model asks you three questions

What do you want to find out?

Which file should it use (from Your files, or one you add)?

Who will read the result?

Add the rule and the check

The rule: what counts? For instance, is an abstention agreement with a “no”?

The check: one result you already know, to test the app against.

The limits: ask the app to say on screen what it cannot answer.

“Show the recommendations my country received on freedom of assembly, by year and by the body that made them, with a link to each record. I know there were 12 in the last UPR cycle: show that count.”
