Inside the Machine · what a model is made of

For a computer, everything is zeros and ones

Text

Every letter is a number, every number is eight bits. Letters outside English (ł, é, ש) take two bytes or more: shown in red.

Image

Move the slider to zoom in, then hover over a pixel.

A video frame

A video is about 30 such images every second.

A model turns each of these into a vector, a long list of numbers (an embedding): text cut into tokens, images into patches. All of it ends up in one space.
